I’m Julia Holzwert, a product and graphic designer focused on UX and UI, with a strong interest in how people interact with digital products, how systems shape behavior, and how design connects technology, communication, and marketing into one coherent experience. HOW I WORK

The work starts with understanding context, constraints, and user needs, and develops through structured design thinking into functional product solutions. Concepts are translated into interfaces that support real behavior, with attention to hierarchy, flow, and visual coherence across touchpoints and platforms.

Collaboration runs across product, development, and strategy teams, ensuring that decisions stay aligned with both user expectations and business goals. Feedback is integrated continuously, allowing concepts to evolve through testing, iteration, and refinement until the system feels consistent and usable in real environments.

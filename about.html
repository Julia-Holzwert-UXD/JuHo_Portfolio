About Julia

Julia Holzwert

Julia is a UX/UI and product designer focused on turning unclear systems into structured, usable and visually coherent digital experiences.

By combining research, interface design, accessibility and visual systems, her work focuses on the moments where users face friction, uncertainty or missed guidance. Each project starts with one question of what needs to become clearer for the person using it

It started a year before COVID. I was in need of presenting for a lot of subjects, so I opened Powerpoint. Where others saw a quick necessity I started designing passionitly.

After my graduation I realised how much fun prototyping & system development was through my UX studies. In 2024 I began creating banners within the Quick-Commerce scene.

When the UX study eventually ended, I realised how much fun web design & development was and started creating my own portfolio from code.

Currently, I am looking for a working student role or internship in UX/UI, product design or digital experience design. I’d love to hear from you.
